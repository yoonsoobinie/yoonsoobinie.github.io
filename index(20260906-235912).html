About

A short academic introduction.

I am a Ph.D. student in Aerospace Engineering and Mechanics at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities. Before joining Minnesota, I received my B.S. in Information and Communication Engineering from Dongguk University in Seoul.

My research background spans cooperative UAV–UGV systems, convex optimization, load balancing, path planning, rotor dynamics and control, and IEEE 802.11ax uplink random access. I enjoy problems where optimization, system constraints, and real-world coordination have to be handled together.

Research

Selected projects and research directions.

01

Cooperative UAV–UGV Parcel Pickup

Designed and analyzed cooperative scheduling and load-balancing methods for parcel-pickup systems involving unmanned aerial and ground vehicles.

Convex optimization · Load balancing · Path planning · IEEE T-ITS
02

IEEE 802.11ax UORA Scheduling

Studied uplink OFDMA random access scheduling and developed a MATLAB-based simulator for adaptive access control based on per-station transmission history.

Wireless communication · IEEE 802.11ax · MATLAB · KICS 2026
03

Rotor Dynamics Modeling with SMC-PIDC Control

Implemented rotor dynamics and SMC-PIDC control models in Simulink and prepared technical reports on the resulting system behavior.

Control · Rotor dynamics · Simulink
04

AI-based Independent Study

Explored AI methods and produced technical reports through an independent study project at Dongguk University.

Artificial intelligence · Technical research
